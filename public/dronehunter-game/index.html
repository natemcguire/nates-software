🎯 DRONE HUNTER 95

An authentic browser arcade shooter inspired by retro Duck Hunt. Defend the meadow from incoming drone waves!

🎮 HOW TO PLAY

Aim & Shoot: Mouse click or tap screen
Reload: Press [R], click reload icon or button
Pause / Resume: Press [P] or [Escape]
Mute Sound: Press [M] or sound button
Start / Next: Press [Space] or [Enter]
Miss Limit: 3 drones escaped = Game Over

⭐ SCORING

+100 pts per drone shot down. -50 pts per escaped drone. +500 pts PERFECT DEFENSE BONUS when every drone in the round is stopped.

🔒 Local-First Privacy: High scores and preferences are saved locally on your device. Zero external tracking or accounts.
