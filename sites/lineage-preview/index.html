Lineage · the viral object

Every fork carries its whole family with it.

When someone forks an app and sells a copy, the money flows back up the tree. That tree is the most interesting thing on the platform — so we made it a thing you can drop into a README, a tweet, or a landing page. It updates itself, and every node is a real maker.

03 · Why it's a growth loop

A share card at every step of the money

Nothing here is a "share to unlock" gimmick. The card is a byproduct of something the maker already did — and it always points back at the tree, where the next person can fork.

1

Fork produces a card

On the first commit of a fork, mint the "I forked {app}" card. The maker shares their version; the link resolves to their node in the tree.

2

First payout produces a card

The first real dollar is the strongest moment. The "first $X" card is catnip for the anti-SaaS crowd — and it names the 70% split without a pitch.

3

The tree pulls people in

Every card links to the embeddable tree. A stranger lands on "12 forks", sees real makers earning, and the obvious next move is Fork it →.
