# Viral / growth ideas (Product Hunt founder lens)

Framing: the product is technically solid but the mechanics are all SUPPLY-side (makers publish,
fork, sell). Virality lives on the DEMAND side and in social loops. Right now a visitor buys/forks
alone and leaves — no reason to return tomorrow, no reason to tell a friend. Ranked by leverage.

## 1. A shareable artifact from every action (highest leverage)
Every meaningful action should produce something the user WANTS to send to a friend (the Nikita Bier
principle). Today nothing marketplace-facing is shareable (only the DYNO benchmark badge exists).
Build:
- **Fork share card** — "I forked {app}, here's my version" with an OG image, on fork success.
- **First-sale moment** — "I made my first ${X} on Nate's Software" card, on first payout.
- **Embeddable lineage tree** — the fork family tree of an app ("400 forks, here's the tree"),
  beautiful + embeddable. The tree IS the viral object and it's on-brand (lineage is the unique idea).
  **Build this first.** It turns every transaction into a growth loop.

## 2. Load the 12:01 AM drop hook (retention)
HOTWIRE's daily drop is a PH-shaped retention mechanic, but nothing PULLS people back: no "your drop
is live in 3h", no drop-time notification/email, no "you're #3 on today's board", no surfaced streak
pressure. The countdown exists; the anticipation loop doesn't. Add: drop-time notifications, "your
rank today", streak pressure made visible, a daily "today's winners" recap.

## 3. Demand-side identity + FOMO
Voting feels anonymous. Add social proof you participate in: "who else forked this", "trending forks
right now", "N bought this in the last hour", a leaderboard of the best MODDERS (not just makers — a
"hunters"-style status for people who fork+improve).

## 4. Make the money story the loud hero
"Buy once, own forever; when your fork sells you keep 70%" is a tweetable anti-SaaS thesis (catnip for
HN/PH right now) but it's buried. Homepage should LEAD with live counters: "$X paid to makers",
"N forks sold today", a real-time payout ticker. Make the money flow visible + visceral.

## 5. Zero-friction first hit
First 10s should be: land → see a cool app running → "fork this + change one thing with AI" → INSTANT
result → "share your version". No signup until they've felt the magic. The wizard now leads with
"run in browser" (good) but the AI-mod moment isn't instant/delightful yet.

## The one to build first
The embeddable, beautiful **lineage-tree share card** + a **share card on fork/sale**. On-brand,
turns transactions into growth loops, and it's the artifact that makes a stranger go "wait, what is
this?" (Prototype as a mock first, like the SLOPSHOP redesign, then implement.)
