# Nate's Software — Brand Marketing Copy (Authored by Grok)

**Own the copy on your disk.**

Nate's Software is a consumer marketplace for shareware you keep. You walk out with the running web app, a clean Git repository, and native binaries for the machines you already sit at: macOS \, Windows \, Linux AppImage, and iOS through TestFlight or sideload.

---

## The Problem

You still pay rent on a calendar you 'bought' last year. The vendor can raise the price, kill the view you used, or go dark on a Tuesday. Your notes live in their warehouse. Export, when they offer it, is a zip of leftovers.

They call this a subscription. You occupy the apartment; they keep the deed. Forking is a terms-of-service violation. Customizing means waiting for a roadmap written in another city. Your photos and lists are 'yours' the way a hotel minibar is yours.

Software learned to be a landlord. The login is the lease. The data is the furniture they kindly let you sit on.

---

## The Solution

A purchase here is a checkout of real artifacts. The web app runs. The Git history is a repository you can clone, not a badge on a marketing page. The binaries launch from your disk.

Want it different? You take your copy to the shop the way you take a car you hold title to. Claude and Codex work on *your* fork, hood open, parts on the bench. You bolt on a feature grown in someone else's copy, then run the result in a runtime whose SQLite file sits next to the binary.

Five unbundled shareware tools. Small on purpose. You own the files.

---

## The 5 Unbundled Tools

### 1. HOTWIRE — Daily drops, scored by use
Titles hit the board the morning someone ships them. You browse what people ran today, mark what you might fork tonight, and skip the rest. Discovery with no featured-partner slot and no synthetic 'hotness' meter. Shareware energy: the good stuff rises because somebody opened it before lunch.
- *White Paper available: Architecture, drop schedules, and transparent popularity metrics.*

### 2. SLOPSHOP — The AI speed shop
You fork a title, tell Claude or Codex what to change, and package the work as a modular feature. Labor happens on source you own. You leave with a build you can run, share, or merge. The shop metaphor is literal: bays, parts, a machine with your name on the title.
- *White Paper available: AST syntax slicing, component dependency graphs, and AI agent handoff.*

### 3. RIG.EXE — The runtime with no ceremony
Runs real builds and previews in disposable, bounded containers. Bring SQLite, Postgres, object storage, flat files, browser storage, or nothing persistent. RIG runs the declared workload and reports real health and resource evidence without choosing the app's database for it.
- *White Paper available: Ephemeral container lifecycle, storage freedom, and evidence-backed OOM recovery.*

### 4. GITSMITH — A bare Git forge that knows what a feature is
You host the repos here. A fork grew a better export, or a calendar view you want; GITSMITH pulls that feature across histories instead of treating every hunk as sacred. Semantic merge for people who fork as a habit. History you can read. Branches you can keep.
- *White Paper available: Bare Git over SSH, sandboxed worktrees, and atomic CAS merge queue.*

### 5. INBOX.EXE — Mail for humans and coding agents
Three panes. People and agents on the left, the thread in the middle, the proposed change on the right. You argue, revise, and approve work here so Git stays for commits. Mail carries 'should we do this.' You stop stuffing a pull request with a conversation that belongs in an inbox.
- *White Paper available: Decentralized mail architecture, cryptographic commit provenance, and agent RBAC.*

## DYNO — The LLM Dynamometer
DYNO measures a complete LLM driving system—model, harness, tools, environment, and execution—on repeatable real-world developer commands. It is a standalone evaluation product, not a benchmark of marketplace apps. Public Street runs are self-reported; Reproduced and Certified results require progressively stronger independent evidence, with Certified Double-Blind reserved for attested private evaluations.
